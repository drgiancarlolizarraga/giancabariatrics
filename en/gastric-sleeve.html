FAQ

What everyone asks.

Am I a candidate for the gastric sleeve?
If your weight is affecting your health or your quality of life, you likely are. In your consultation we review your case and tell you which path is best for you.
How much does it cost?
It depends on your case. In your consultation we give you the exact price, and we offer monthly payments from $199 USD so cost doesn't stop your change.
Is it safe?
It's one of the most performed bariatric procedures in the world. It's done in a certified hospital, and in your consultation we answer every question so you can decide with peace of mind.
How soon can I get back to normal life?
Most patients return to light activities in 7 to 10 days and to their routine in 3 to 4 weeks. From there, the change becomes more noticeable every week.
Do you accept health insurance?
We have agreements with insurance companies. Send us the name of your insurer and we'll let you know if it applies to your case.
I live in the U.S. Can you treat me?
Yes. Many patients come from San Diego and other cities. You can have your consultation by video call, and we'll help you plan your visit to Tijuana.
How do I book my consultation?
Message us on WhatsApp or email us at drgiancarlolizarraga@gmail.com. Your consultation is free.
