FAQ

What everyone asks.

Am I a candidate for surgery?
If your weight is affecting your health or your quality of life, you very likely are. In your consultation we review your case and I'll tell you which path is best for you: surgery, GLP-1 treatment, or neither.
How much does it cost?
It depends on your case and the treatment you need. In your consultation we give you the exact price and financing options, so cost doesn't have to hold you back.
How soon will I notice a change?
Many patients notice it within the first few weeks: their clothes fit differently, they have more energy and they feel calmer around food. Every body moves at its own pace, and we track your progress at your follow-up visits.
I live in the U.S. Can you treat me?
Yes. Many of our patients come from San Diego and other U.S. cities. We care for you in English or Spanish and help you plan your visit to Tijuana.
Do you accept health insurance?
We have agreements with insurance companies. Send us the name of your insurer and we'll let you know if it applies to your case.
How do I book my consultation?
Message us on WhatsApp or, if you prefer, email us at drgiancarlolizarraga@gmail.com and we'll send you available times. Your consultation is free.
