In compliance with Mexico's Federal Law on the Protection of Personal Data Held by Private Parties (LFPDPPP) and its Regulations, we provide this Privacy Notice to inform you how we handle the personal data you share with us.

1. Identity and address of the data controller

The party responsible for processing your personal data is Dr. Giancarlo Lizárraga, operating under the trade name Gianca Bariatrics, located at Hospital Blue, Office 917, Vía Rápida Oriente 9750, Zona Urbana Río, Tijuana, Baja California, Mexico.

2. Personal data we collect

For the purposes described in this Notice, we may collect the following categories of data:

Identification and contact data

  • Full name
  • Age and date of birth
  • Phone number (mobile and landline)
  • Email address
  • City and country of residence

Sensitive personal data (health data)

As part of the medical care we provide, we may collect sensitive data related to your health, such as:

  • Weight, height and history of obesity
  • Personal and family medical history
  • Current conditions and medications you take
  • Results of clinical tests and lab work
  • Medical images (clinical photographs, imaging studies)

Under the LFPDPPP, sensitive data requires your express consent, which is understood to be given when you voluntarily provide this information for your medical care.

3. Purposes of processing

Your personal data will be used for the following purposes:

Primary purposes (necessary for the service)

  • Providing medical care, clinical evaluation and surgical services
  • Creating and maintaining your medical record
  • Coordinating consultations, appointments and post-operative follow-up
  • Managing payment plans, financing and invoicing
  • Complying with applicable legal, tax and health regulations
  • Communicating with you electronically (WhatsApp, email, phone calls)

Secondary purposes (not necessary for the service)

  • Sending information about new services, promotions or events
  • Satisfaction surveys and continuous improvement
  • Use of testimonials or results for educational or promotional purposes (only with your additional written consent)

If you do not want your data used for secondary purposes, you may let us know at any time through the channels listed in section 8.

4. Transfer of personal data

Your personal data may be shared with:

  • Hospitals and medical centers where your procedure takes place, solely for its clinical and administrative purposes
  • Healthcare professionals involved in your care (anesthesiologists, nutritionists, psychologists, laboratories)
  • Insurance companies we have agreements with, only when you choose to use your medical insurance and for the related paperwork
  • Health or judicial authorities when legally required
  • Technology service providers (medical management systems, communication platforms) under strict confidentiality agreements

We do not sell, trade or transfer your personal data to third parties for purposes other than those described in this Notice.

5. How we limit the use or disclosure of your data

We implement administrative, technical and physical measures to protect your personal data against damage, loss, alteration, destruction or unauthorized use. These measures include:

  • Access to medical records restricted to authorized personnel only
  • Secure storage of data in protected systems
  • Staff training on data protection
  • Confidentiality agreements with collaborators and providers

6. ARCO rights

As the owner of your data, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Rectify it when it is inaccurate or incomplete
  • Cancel it when you consider it is no longer necessary for the stated purposes
  • Object to the processing of your data for specific purposes

You may also revoke your consent at any time, and limit the use or disclosure of your information.

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to improve your browsing experience, analyze traffic and personalize content. We also use tracking pixels (Meta Pixel) to measure the effectiveness of our advertising campaigns.

You can set your browser to disable cookies; however, this may affect how the site works.
